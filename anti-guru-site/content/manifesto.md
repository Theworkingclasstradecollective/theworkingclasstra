You have a day job, a rent to pay and about forty minutes of attention left at the end of the day. Every industry that sells certainty knows it. This is what we believe, and what we are building instead.

## 1. No gods, no masters

Nobody makes your trades for you. Nobody lifts your weights for you. That includes us. A guru is a subscription with a face. If someone needs you to believe, they are selling you their income, not your results.

We do not want followers. We want readers who check our arithmetic.

## 2. The machine does not love you

Brokers earn when you click. Gyms earn when you sign and never show up. Supplement brands earn when you stack. Signal rooms earn when you renew. None of them need you to win, get strong or stay healthy.

Follow the money first. Then read the claim.

## 3. Cold calculations

Most "secrets" are arithmetic. So we publish the arithmetic: made-up numbers, worked one step at a time, formula after you have seen the numbers.

- Count what it costs. Every trade has a price tag. Every body has a recovery bill.
- Write the rules before the session. Not after, when you already know the result.
- Test on your own log. 100 trades. 24 workouts. If your numbers say no, the answer is no.

A feeling is not a number. A black box is not a plan. A screenshot is a highlight reel.

## 4. Anti-subscription sovereignty

Your tools should still work when the company behind them disappears.

- **Paper and CSV beat apps.** A spreadsheet you own outlives any dashboard you rent.
- **Sixty lines of Python beat a "platform".** If you can read it, you can trust it, and you can fix it.
- **A floor, a wall and a bar** are enough to start. No membership, no brand, no body type required.
- **Copyleft, always.** Our manuals are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Copy them, translate them, print them, sell them, as long as you credit us and keep them free for the next person.

If it only works while you keep paying, it works for the seller.

## 5. Survive first

A dead account earns nothing. A strong person with wrecked joints is a weak person. Muscle is fast, tendons are slow, and the market does not care about your mood.

Position size, daily locks, deloads and rest weeks are not a break from the program. They are the program.

## 6. Be honest about the odds

Regulators in many places force brokers to publish how many retail accounts lose money on leveraged products, and the published share is usually well over half. Overuse injuries often follow a sudden jump in work, not steady work.

Trading is not a ticket out of your job. Strength is not a personality. Both are ways to get through a hard job, a long life and a bad week with something left in the tank.

A FAIL on a checklist is not an insult. It is a to-do item.

## 7. Mutual aid

You do not have to do this alone, and you do not need a leader to do it together.

- Share rules, not calls. Share routines, not cults.
- Find a journal buddy. Swap compliance scores, not profit or looks.
- Be kind to the person who just blew up an account or got hurt. It happens to careful people too.
- Pass the math on. Every formula we print is yours to hand to a friend.

## 8. What we are not

We are not your advisor, your coach, your doctor or your signal room. Nothing we publish is financial or medical advice. Everything is a hypothesis to test. If any of this stops feeling like a tool and starts feeling like a compulsion, step away and talk to someone you trust or a professional. No account and no routine is worth that.
