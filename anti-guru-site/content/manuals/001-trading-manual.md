Brokers, platforms and course sellers make money when you trade a lot. They do not need you to win. This manual treats trading like a boring little shop: a small edge, strict limits, and every number written down.

You get the arithmetic in plain words, then the formula, worked on a made-up **$1,000 account**:

- **The cost of every trade.** Spread, slippage and commission, in dollars.
- **Naked price action.** Swings, stop piles, force and gaps. No indicators.
- **Cold math.** Position size, expectancy, break-even win rate and drawdown recovery.
- **Execution rules.** YES / NO steps and a PASS / FAIL audit at the end of every chapter.
- **Defense.** Daily and weekly locks, a drawdown table, and a journal you can actually keep.

Everything in it is a hypothesis to test, not a promise. The book asks for **100 demo trades** before you risk a cent. If your own numbers show no profit after costs, the system fails and you keep your cash. That is a win.

Free templates and scripts that go with the book (journal CSV, 50-trade log, expectancy script) are on the [open resources page](/resources/#book-001).
