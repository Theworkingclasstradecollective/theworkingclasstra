Gyms sell memberships, supplement brands sell stacks, and influencers sell toughness. None of them need your joints to last. This manual points the same cold method at your body: count the work, cap the jumps, write the session down.

All you need is a floor, a wall and a sturdy bar. Inside:

- **Five patterns, nothing else.** Push, pull, hinge, squat, core, each with a five-rung leverage ladder. Make a move harder by changing the angle, the tempo or the lever, never by chasing ego.
- **Cold math.** One workload formula, a **spike ratio** that keeps weekly jumps near 10%, and recovery rules for shift work, heavy jobs and desk jobs.
- **Joint longevity.** A five-minute prep block and a joint-by-joint table, including when to get checked.
- **Execution rules.** Five gates before the first set, a PASS / FAIL audit after every chapter.
- **Defense.** Circuit breakers, deloads and a return plan after a layoff or an injury.

Everything in it is a hypothesis to test on your own body. Run the plan for **12 weeks and at least 24 logged workouts** before you judge it. If your log says you are not getting stronger, or your joints are getting worse, the plan changes. That is a win.
